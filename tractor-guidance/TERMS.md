## 7. Data, privacy, maps, and availability

Recorded GPS tracks, properties, regions, saved paths, and drive sessions are designed to remain in the local browser/device unless the user explicitly exports them. Map imagery requests to allowlisted map providers can reveal the approximate area currently being viewed. Address text is sent to the U.S. Census geocoder only when address search is used.

Local browser storage can be cleared, corrupted, or lost. Device failure, browser updates, permission changes, network outages, map-provider outages, or other events can also make saved data or map content unavailable. Export important data when loss would matter.
