# Challenge content register — 0.1.2

Updated: 2026-10-26 (America/Chicago). Generated by `node scripts/write-challenge-register.mjs`. Edit original content in `challenges.js`; do not alter the immutable legacy definitions in `catalog.js`.

## Coverage and limits

20 of 204 built-in skills have 580 concrete objectives: 29 per path, spanning all 14 categories. Levels 30–50 combine discipline-specific evidence context with editorial expert/national/historic achievement milestones. The other 184 built-in skills and custom skills retain the general rank framework. No path has an independently validated numerical conversion from external results to RPG levels.

Each expanded path has three approaches: core demonstration, a practical brief, or changed conditions. These alter the visible objective, not the required standing. Changed conditions never require additional hazard. Three discipline-specific quality criteria plus a rank-band criterion apply below level 30; four evidence criteria apply at higher ranks. These criteria are intentionally absent from the roadmap and first-attempt UI until the user claims completion. Safety and required scope are visible beforehand.

An ordinary lifelong amateur may remain in levels 20–29. Level 30 requires recognized expertise or substantive local/regional achievement; level 40 requires leading national/international standing. Level 50 requires sustained historic best-in-world, open-age achievement or a defensible domain equivalent, not a qualification, participation or age-group title. Some domains have no defensible world ranking. They remain self-attested editorial ambitions, not objectively verified best-in-world claims.

The authors created these objectives for Human RPG. References provide professional, qualification or competition context; they do not endorse the app. Do not copy entire licensed rubrics into this catalog or imply official SFIA/CEFR/WorldSkills alignment. See [RANKINGS.md](RANKINGS.md) for every skill's ranking evidence and calculator limits. This update adds no new percentile calculator.

## Saved challenge contract

New attempts store a complete format-1 snapshot: skill, rank, selected approach, edition, title, objective, instructions, four scoring criteria and source-context IDs/notes. Read the snapshot for pending and passed attempts. Catalog updates must not rewrite it. Keep UI output escaped. Reference IDs resolve against trusted local metadata; a backup cannot supply arbitrary reference URLs.

Attempts without a snapshot use the exact 0.1.0/0.1.1 legacy generator. They are not silently upgraded; no new requirements are imposed on unfinished or passed attempts. XP thresholds, permanent gateways and reward idempotence are unchanged. Existing purchase permissions keep their recorded price and title.

The optional snapshot does not change state schema 1 or archive format 1. ZIP/JSON preserve it. Version 0.1.1 can parse a snapshot-bearing state but does not display its new terms; review and score new attempts only in 0.1.2 or later. Keep a backup before switching builds. New binary photos still require 0.1.1+. A legacy JSON file changes the photo encoding, not the challenge definition. No data migration or reset is performed.

The fifty-level roadmap is read-only. It displays current-edition objectives, safety, evidence scope, prospective XP, gear and income-based purchase permission. It never adds an attempt, grants a reward, spends credits or reveals the scoring criteria. Reward previews use current income and catalog suggestions; already-awarded rewards remain unchanged.
